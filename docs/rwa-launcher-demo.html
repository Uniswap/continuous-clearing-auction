RWA Launcher — Interactive Demo

A uniform-price reverse auction on a discount: an issuer pre-builds a two-sided concentrated LP position and sells fractional shares of it. Bidders accept a discount off NAV; everyone clears at one marginal discount. Adjust the thresholds and bids, then end the auction.

Illustrative demo of the design in docs/RWALauncherSpec.md. Token unit prices and fees are placeholders for visualization only. 1 share = $1 of position notional.
